# Where everything comes from

The files mix my own work with the publisher's. Crediting a source does not grant rights in it. Only the material in the first section is mine to license. All files are in `files/`.

## My own work

| File | Notes |
|---|---|
| `voting-summary.pdf` | A summary of the voting rules in my wording. It contains no publisher art. |
| `host-round-guide.pdf` | A walk-through of one Dilemma round in my wording. It contains no publisher art. |
| `README.md` | What I sent my group and when, based on our group chat. |

My own text also appears in the welcome and the symbols overview. Those files are listed under publisher content, because they also contain the publisher's art or icons.

## Publisher content

The publisher's text, art and icons in these files are © Horrible Games SRL. The originals come with the game or from the [publisher's website](https://horribleguild.com/eu/product/the-queens-dilemma/). The table says which parts are mine.

| File | Origin | What I changed |
|---|---|---|
| `character-choice.pdf` | The six characters' descriptions and panels from Rulebook v15, pp. 8–9, and three ideology emblems from publisher material. I didn't record exactly where the emblems came from. | Descriptions translated into German, new layout, and a German cover page and ideology page. |
| `diaries.pdf` | The text of the six character Diaries, transcribed word for word and checked against scans of the printed Diaries. The portraits and crests are the publisher's. | New layout, plus a short German cover page. |
| `prologue.pdf` | "War in Konfurtah", Rulebook v15, p. 13. The text is word for word. | New layout, plus German headings. |
| `disclaimer.jpg` | The disclaimer from the front of Rulebook v15. The text is word for word. | New layout, plus German headings. |
| `welcome.pdf` | My own introduction to the rules, in my wording. It uses three ideology emblems from publisher material and eight symbols from the rulebook. | Everything except the emblems and symbols is mine. |
| `map-labels.jpg` | The twelve region labels, cut from the [publisher's map image](https://horribleguild.com/eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/qd_map_singola_v29-1.png). | Cropped, enlarged and arranged in a grid, with a source line. |
| `symbols.pdf` | My explanation of the game's symbols, with 45 symbols cut from Rulebook v15, pp. 41–49. | Everything except the symbols is mine. |
| `rulebook-agenda-cards.pdf` | Rulebook v15, pp. 46–47, cut from the PDF. | Cropped, with a source line on each page. A ship illustration is left out. |
| `rulebook-chronicle-cards.pdf` | Rulebook v15, pp. 44–45 and all of p. 19 (setup step 8), cut from the PDF. | pp. 44–45 are cropped. Every page has a source line. |
| `rulebook-pass-actions.pdf` | Rulebook v15, pp. 24, 25 and 28, cut from the PDF. | Cropped, with a source line on each page. On p. 25 I masked neighbouring art. On p. 28 I added the line "Later, in the cleanup phase:". |

The full rulebook is not included. Please get it from the publisher.
