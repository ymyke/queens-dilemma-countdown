# The Queen's Dilemma: Countdown to the First Game

These are free, unofficial fan materials for hosting the legacy board game *The Queen's Dilemma*. The publisher has not endorsed them, and you need your own copy of the game.

I hosted a group of six players. We didn't want to spend the first evening reading rules, so in the days before the first game I sent one small piece a day to our WhatsApp group. The aim was that everyone would arrive knowing their character, the map and how a vote works. My players told me they appreciated this a lot.

The tables below show what I sent and when, counted in days from the first game. Our second game was a week after the first. All files are in [`files/`](files/). Most of them are in German and use the game's English terms, because we played the English edition. The publisher's texts stay in English.

## What you need from the publisher

The rulebook is on the [publisher's product page](https://horribleguild.com/eu/product/the-queens-dilemma/), and the [Lore Compendium](https://horribleguild.com/eu/the-queens-dilemma-lore-compendium/) is on their website. Neither is included here. My texts follow Rulebook v15, and where they disagree with the rulebook, the rulebook is right.

## How the files were made

I made the files with an AI assistant (Claude) and checked them against the rulebook, partly with further AI review passes. That includes the transcriptions of the publisher's texts. They can still contain errors, so please report any you find as an issue.

Most files were printed from an HTML page in [`source/`](source/). To change, extend or translate one, give its HTML page to an AI assistant, open the result in a browser and print it to PDF. The rulebook extracts and the map labels have no HTML page.
